## The owner's answers, and the gap they name

Three decisions were settled on 2026-10-21, and a decision that is not written
down is a conversation that will be re-litigated.

The gap: ADR 0007 is the environment agents operate and extend, and the Forge
direction. These three are not that.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **ADR 0008 — The product has five domains.** It says what the five are,
      what the three rules are, that the map is declarative, and what it costs.
      It records the three judgement calls and their reasons, so a later reader
      does not have to re-derive them.
- [ ] **ADR 0009 — MiniMax Code is a specification, not a dependency.** It
      says what the licence permits and what it does not, what was taken and
      what was ignored, that the patterns split by boundary, and that there is
      no desktop application.
- [ ] **ADR 0010 — Self-improvement without self-dependence.** It says the two
      planes, the seven boundaries, and the one link each way between the
      repository's record and the instance's.
- [ ] **Each record says what it rejected**, because a decision without its
      alternatives is a preference, and a preference is not a decision.
- [ ] **Each record follows the house shape**: context, decision,
      consequences.

The decision records are the durable form of a session. The session's own
record — what was asked, what was answered, and in what order — is in
`.factory/INTAKE.md`, and it is written so that a reader can see which answer
produced which decision.
