## The problem

`PRODUCT.md` §11 still says the direction that would fix the accent is
**"Desk (B), it is recommended rather than decided, and the owner has one word
to say about it"** (`PRODUCT.md:280`).

The owner said their word at 06:01: **D — Forge**. ADR 0007 says Forge, with
the date and the history. The direction is decided, and **the product record
is the last place still telling the old story** — and the product record is
the document a new reader opens first.

A record that states the opposite of the decision is the one thing it cannot
carry.
