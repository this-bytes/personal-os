## The problem

Two published surfaces have **no test at all**.

1. `GET /api/autonomy-policy` — `apps/server/src/api/autonomy.ts`.
2. The `autonomy.read` MCP tool — granted in `packages/contracts/src/mcp.ts`,
   served in `apps/server/src/modules/mcp/server.ts`.

WI-096 published both in `CONTRACTS.md` and found this gap, and left it open.
WI-096's own finding was that the publication and the implementation were never
checked against each other by anything that runs; this item is the check.

**The contract is the truth and the code is the defect.** The test asserts the
shape `CONTRACTS.md` publishes — the example body at `CONTRACTS.md:1402-1421`
and the interface at `:1378-1396` — not the shape the implementation happens to
answer with. Where the two disagree, that is a **finding**: it is reported, not
fixed, and the test is not weakened to make it pass.

## Why now

WI-096 landed the publication on this branch and nothing exercises it. A
published surface with no test is a claim, not a contract: the next change to
the resolver or the route moves the published shape without anything failing.
This item makes the publication falsifiable.
