## What the check must hold

**The four sentences WI-106's acceptance turned true:**

1. **`docs/dependency-licences.json` → `reads.evidence`** opens _"Each third-party entry
   records the file its licence was read from and the evidence that reading rests
   on…"_, and **"every entry" appears nowhere in it**.
2. **`PRODUCT.md:290-294`** says _"one entry for every package in the lockfile"_, and the
   record holds **494** — the lockfile's **494 distinct packages**, of 500 non-root entries,
   the six workspaces being listed twice.
3. **The 488 third-party entries each carry `source.file`, `source.evidence` and
   `source.readOn`**, and the six first-party workspaces are named as carrying no source
   (`source: null` 6/6, `spdxId: null` 6/6, a `firstParty` object 6/6).
4. **"the lines of that file that identify the licence" is gone**, and **"the evidence that
   identifies it"** is true of all three shapes — 375 licence-file, 16 package-json-licence
   field, 97 published-licence identifier.

**And the counts, because a count that drifts is the same defect:** 375 / 16 / 97 / 6 =
**494**, `summary` 375/16/97, **488** third-party, **11** distinct `spdxId`, `familiesRead`
**11 rows summing to 488**, and the record's **494** `lockPath`s are exactly the 494 paths
under `node_modules/` — **0 missing, 0 extra**.

**And the check must hold itself to failing.** **A check that only ever passes is not a
check.** It must go red on a copy of the record whose sentences have drifted back — the
shape WI-122's pin takes, and the shape this factory has learned the hard way.
