## The gap it names

The effective autonomy policy is composed from three domains' rows: the
workspace's policy, the goal's policy, and the conversation's own limits. The
resolver lives in `apps/server/src/modules/autonomy/service.ts`, and the
`autonomy` module is Work's — the policy says what work may proceed.

**The one reach across is the conversation's row.** The workspace's policy is
Substrate's, and every domain may use Substrate; the goal's policy is Work's
own row. The conversation's limits are Interaction's, and the Work domain
reads them to finish the composition.

**This one is a table read rather than an import**, which is why the manifest
keeps it in a separate list: proving that a module reads only its own tables
needs a data-flow analysis this repository does not have, and a rule nothing
verifies is a claim.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **The effective policy is composed by the one module that may read
      across.** The `capabilities` module is the sanctioned crossing; the
      resolver takes the conversation's limits as an argument instead of
      reading the conversation's row.
- [ ] **The domain map's table claims stop being crossed**, and the shared
      table read is removed from the manifest.
- [ ] **The policy that is enforced does not change.** The same workspace, goal
      and conversation produce the same effective policy, the worker enforces
      the same policy, and the `autonomy.read` MCP tool publishes the same
      policy. WI-082 and WI-095 own that behaviour, and this item does not
      change it.
- [ ] **`docs/domains.md` is updated.**

## Not in scope

- **The policy's shape.** The workspace, goal and conversation layers, and what
  each one may forbid, are published in `CONTRACTS.md` and owned by WI-096.
  This item is about where the conversation's row is read from.
- **The enforcement points.** The worker and the MCP surface stay where they
  are. The worker reaches the resolver through the server's runtime export, and
  that is not a domain crossing: the worker is not a domain in the map.

## Notes

**This item and WI-059 are the same shape.** WI-059 removes the Resources →
Work crossing by reading the ledger through the capability API. This one
removes the Work → Interaction crossing the same way: the read happens in the
module that is allowed to make it, and the domain that needed it asks.

**The map's rule 3 is not a prohibition on a policy that spans domains.** It is
a prohibition on a reach-across that is hidden. A policy composed from three
domains' facts is a real thing, and the honest form is a declared crossing with
an owner and a way out — which is what this item is.
