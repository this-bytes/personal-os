## The problem

**Two of the six kinds can never be agent-authored, and the contract says so as
a fact about the product.** `CONTRACTS.md:1272-1280`:

> **Two of the six kinds can never be agent-authored.** The `automation` and
> `task` projections hardcode `createdBy: 'system'`
> (`apps/server/src/modules/resources/service.ts:170`, `:260`), and their tables
> have no `created_by` column at all
> (`apps/server/src/db/schema.ts:385-401`, `:305-330`) — the value is invented
> at projection time and nothing is stored. An agent-created automation or task
> is recorded as authored by `'system'`. **The first measure is therefore a
> lower bound**: those resources sit in the denominator and can never sit in
> the count, so the ratio is a share over four of the six kinds, reported over
> all six.

**That is true today, and it is true in the wrong tense.** WI-033's code will
give those two kinds a real author, and when it does, this paragraph is a
falsehood about the product — the class WI-065 exists for.

**And the contract is what WI-033 implements against, so it has to be written
first.** WI-034 was asked to publish the authorship shape and **deliberately did
not**: publishing it before the implementation would be publishing a proposal,
because whether the two kinds gain a column or the author is read from where it
already lives is settled by the implementation. That reasoning is right, and it
leaves a hole: **WI-033's fourth criterion refers the shape to the contract, and
the contract does not have it.** So this item publishes the part that _can_ be
published — and it is most of it.

**What can be published is the field, and the field is already published.** A
resource's `createdBy` is the published shape, for all six kinds, and the
authorship entry at `CONTRACTS.md:1254` already says what counts as an agent's
(`createdBy` carries the `agt_` form; `'owner'` and `'system'` are not agents).
**The storage is the implementation's business and this item does not publish
it.** What is missing is only the statement that the two kinds' published
resources **will** carry the same field the other four carry, in the house form:
the code does not do it yet, the implementation is WI-033, and a client must
treat those two kinds' author as absent until it lands.

## The house form, and where it comes from

**WI-030 published the template record ahead of its implementation, in exactly
this shape**, and the lead's ruling at the time was that leading means something
in both directions. The form is: _"The code does not implement this yet… the
implementation is WI-nnn, and the shapes above are what it has to reach. A
client written against this entry must treat it as absent until it lands."_
