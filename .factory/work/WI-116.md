## The finding

**The pin that holds the boundary rule's copies does not hold the strategy record's copy.**
Measured on the published tip, `apps/web/src/__tests__/forge-briefs.test.ts`:

- **`RULE_COPIES` at `:494-498` holds three copies** — `01-rooms-and-surfaces.md`,
  `05-needs-you-rail.md` and `README.md`.
- **`docs/strategy.md` is not among them.**
- **And the strategy record carries the rule**, quoted at **`docs/strategy.md:265-270`**:
  _"If it is a picture of the system, it is in the orientation room. If it is a place where
  work is inspected or decided, it is in the execution room — with two declared exceptions,
  the Needs-you rail and the constellation, which carry decisions in the orientation room
  because a decision is taken where the list is."_

**So the strategy record's copy can drift and nothing notices** — and WI-112's acceptance
rests on that copy being the rule.

## What the check must hold — measured on today's tree

- **The direction document's copy:** `prototypes/DIRECTION.md:53-57`, read out of the
  emphasised form by the pin's own `publishedRule()` (`:469-477`).
- **The strategy record's copy:** `docs/strategy.md:265-270`.

- **Both declared exceptions are present** — _"the Needs-you rail"_ and _"the
  constellation"_ — and **"one declared exception" is in neither.**

## Scope

**One file: `apps/web/src/__tests__/forge-briefs.test.ts`, and nothing else.**
`docs/strategy.md` is **read, never edited** — the record is true now, and the work is the
check that keeps it true.

**The copy must be found by its content, not by a line number.** `docs/strategy.md` is the
peer session's live file for **WI-119**, and a pin that asserted on a fixed line range would
turn red when another item's edit moved it — **a check that breaks on an unrelated edit is
a check that gets deleted.** The pin already reads the rule out of the direction document
once and holds every copy to it; **the strategy record's copy is one more entry in that
list, read the same way.**
