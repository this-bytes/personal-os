# WI-102 — The daily spend cap is enforced, so the published limits are all true

**Owner:** Cog
**Verifier:** Probe
**State:** implement — landed 2026-10-22 by Cog, on branch
`wi/102-daily-spend-enforcement`, built on `origin/wi/096-publish-autonomy-contract`
(WI-082's enforcement and WI-096's publication of the policy) with
`origin/main` merged in.

**The code half of the autonomy policy, finished.** WI-082 made the policy
enforceable and WI-096 published it; the daily spend cap was resolved,
published and enforced nowhere, which made the published sentence _"The limits,
the allowed actions and the approvals are enforced."_ false about one of the
limits it names. The attack recorded it as finding F6 (LOW); it is not LOW,
because the repository's newest gate — `scripts/verify-contract-status.ts`,
added by WI-091 — exists to stop the contract claiming something the tree does
not do.

**What landed.** The daily cap is enforced at the same choke point as the
chain's spend limit, and the day is a UTC day.

- `apps/worker/src/executor.ts` — `limitRefusal()` takes the workspace's spend
  for the day and refuses a turn that has no daily budget left, in the same
  message form as the turn and chain-spend refusals and with the same
  `autonomyPolicyLevelPhrase(policy.sources.dailySpendUnits)` attribution, so a
  client can tell which policy decided the cap.
- `apps/worker/src/executor.ts` — `dailySpendUnits()` is the measure: the sum
  of the spend units on the executions of that workspace since 00:00 UTC. It is
  a **query over the executions the product already keeps**, not a counter
  incremented on some code path — a measure is a query over the data the
  product keeps. **No new table, no new column and no migration.**
- `apps/worker/src/__tests__/autonomy-limits.test.ts` — seven cases, added to
  the file WI-082 put in the worker's lane. **No existing case was weakened or
  removed.**

**The refusal, word for word:**

> The turn did not start: the autonomy policy's daily spend limit of 10 units is
> reached for this workspace since 00:00 UTC today. The limit comes from the
> workspace's policy.

**Zero means no cap**, the same convention `maxSpendUnits` uses and the same
one the resolver reads it by: a policy with `dailySpendUnits: 0` imposes no
daily cap, and the two agree.

**The cap is a cap on the workspace.** The measure is the workspace's spend
across every conversation and every goal in it, and not one chain's — that is
the whole difference between a daily cap and `maxSpendUnits`, which is per
chain. Another workspace's spend does not count.

## The contract change Wren has to publish

**`CONTRACTS.md` is Wren's file and this item did not touch it.** The change
needed is in the **autonomy policy** entry, in the paragraph that begins
_"**The limits, the allowed actions and the approvals are enforced.**"_
(`CONTRACTS.md:899-904` on the branch this lands on).

**1. Replace this sentence:**

> The worker holds a turn to the effective policy: the chain's turn count and
> its spend before the turn starts, the duration as the deadline while it runs,
> and every action the agent announces.

**with:**

> The worker holds a turn to the effective policy: the chain's turn count and
> its spend, and the workspace's spend for the day, before the turn starts, the
> duration as the deadline while it runs, and every action the agent announces.

**2. Add this paragraph after that one:**

> **A daily cap is a cap on the workspace.** The workspace's spend for the day
> is the sum of the spend units on the executions of that workspace since 00:00
> UTC, and it is the same day for every workspace — the system stores ISO
> timestamps everywhere and has no per-workspace timezone. A daily cap of zero
> is no cap, as it is for the chain's spend limit. The cap applies across the
> workspace's conversations and goals, and not per chain, which is the whole
> difference between a daily cap and `maxSpendUnits`.

**3. Nothing else changes.** The published shape, the route and the tool are
unchanged: `dailySpendUnits` was already published, and this item changes no
published shape. The migration is additive — there is nothing to migrate, no
record is reclassified and no existing route changes.

## What this item does not do, and what it found

- **The cap binds a conversation turn.** The brief put the daily cap at the
  same choke point as `maxSpendUnits`, and that is where it is. A **factory
  task** and an **automation run** resolve a policy and are held to its
  duration and its allowed actions, but they are not held to the daily cap, and
  a **goal's own** `dailySpendUnits` is therefore resolved and published and
  enforced nowhere — the same status the goal's chain limits have today
  (the goal path has no chain, so it checks no chain limit). **This is a
  finding, not a change**: the item's scope and its _not in scope_ list are the
  delegation path, and the goal path's chain limits are not this item's
  business. It is the same defect one level in, and it wants its own item.
- **The spend units are zero on every execution today.** The column exists,
  it is created at zero, and **nothing in the tree writes it** — so the
  workspace's spend for the day is zero, and the cap cannot bite until
  something records what a turn costs. The enforcement is real and the measure
  is the published one (`SUM(executions.spend_units)`, reported and never
  computed, the rule WI-086's evidence read already follows); what the cap is
  measured against is the number the product records, and the product records
  none. **That is the finding that matters**, and it is a separate item:
  measuring what a turn costs is a new feature, not this one.
- **The query scans the workspace's executions.** `executions_workspace_idx`
  covers the workspace filter; the day filter is applied to the rows that
  index returns. The number of executions in a workspace is the number of
  turns it has run, so the scan grows with the workspace's history. The
  product's own rule — a measure is a query over the data the product keeps —
  is the rule this follows, and a counter table would be a different product
  decision.

## The test

`apps/worker/src/__tests__/autonomy-limits.test.ts` — the file WI-082 put in
the worker's lane, extended and not weakened. Seven cases: a turn refused
because the daily cap is reached; a turn allowed while under the cap; a cap of
zero imposing no limit; a cap that spans conversations within one workspace; a
cap that does not count another workspace's spend; the spend recorded at the
first minute of the UTC day counting; and yesterday's spend not counting.

**The pins were shown to bite.** Three mutations of the implementation were
run against the file: refusing the check always (three cases fail), dropping
the day filter (the yesterday case fails) and dropping the workspace filter
(the other-workspace case fails). The mutations were reverted and the file is
green.
