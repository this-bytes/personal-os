## Problem

Q6 is a decision and nothing implements it. The two measures the owner
settled — the share of shipped features authored by agents inside the
instance, and the share of routine operations completed without the owner
touching code — are never computed and never shown.

**The premise this item was sized on is half false, and Chief corrected it on
2026-10-21** after Probe's WI-011 review. The item said "the data is already
there: `resources.createdBy` separates an agent's authorship from the owner's,
and `auditLog` records the actor of every action." What is actually true:

- `resources.createdBy` separates the two for **four of the six** resource
  kinds. Automations and tasks **hardcode `createdBy: 'system'`**
  (`apps/server/src/modules/resources/service.ts:170`, `:260`) and their
  tables have no `createdBy` column at all
  (`apps/server/src/db/schema.ts:385-401`, `:305-330`). An agent-created
  automation or task is recorded as created by `'system'`, so the first
  measure is **answered wrongly** for those two kinds, not merely uncomputed.
- `auditLog` does **not** record the actor of every action. Chief counted the
  mutating routes route by route: 34 of them, 20 with a route-level `audit()`
  call, two more writing from the service layer (`POST
/api/approvals/:id/decide`, `PATCH /api/tasks/:id`), and **twelve with no
  audit entry at all**. The second measure, read from `auditLog`, would count
  a trail that does not cover a third of what the owner does.

So this item is no longer "compute two numbers from data the product already
keeps". It is "compute two numbers honestly, which means deciding what to do
about two kinds whose author is hardcoded and a third of the routes that write
no entry". The owner's nod on the published contract is still owed, and it
should be sought on this premise rather than the old one.

This is the cheapest item on the list, and it is the one that makes the
product honest about itself. It is also one of the two settled decisions
that had no work item behind them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One read-only route returns both measures, each as a count with its
      denominator alongside the ratio.
- [ ] The route answers for one workspace and follows the same auth and
      workspace rules as every other route.
- [ ] When there is no denominator, the ratio is `null` and not `0`. The
      count is still returned. A panel that greets its owner with 0% reads as
      broken, and the product's first report on itself must not be a lie by
      omission.
- [ ] The band on the home shows the counts, and shows a ratio only where
      there is a denominator. It sits after "what needs me", not before it.
- [ ] The measures are computed by querying the authorship columns and the
      audit trail, not by a counter that is incremented on some code path —
      a counter cannot answer "of the things that exist, how many were made
      by an agent".
- [ ] The route has a `CONTRACTS.md` entry, and the entry lands before the
      route ships.
- [ ] A test proves each measure from known data: an agent-authored resource
      counts as agent-authored, an owner-created one does not, and a zero
      denominator produces a count and a null ratio.

## Not in scope

- **No gauge, no sparkline, no history.** This counts; it does not chart.
- **No new persistence.** A count is a query over data the product already
  keeps. A table of historical measures is a different item.
- **No change to how the measures are defined.** The owner's definitions are
  settled. What is open is exactly what counts as "authored by an agent",
  and this item states its reading in the report so the owner can see it
  and correct it.

## Chief's rulings on this item — 2026-10-21

**The scope does not widen.** WI-030's report flagged the obvious risk: the
second measure — the share of operations done without the owner touching code
— will read as near-zero, because **the MCP surface writes no audit entry at
all** (`7c43464` records it), so the operations an agent actually performs are
invisible to the trail, and every audited operation is an owner operation
except the worker's own `unknown` records. **That gap is
[WI-033](WI-033.md)'s** — "the audit trail records what happened, not that
something happened" — with [WI-034](WI-034.md) publishing the rules it
implements. This item implements the two measures against the published reading
and publishes the limits; it does not widen the audit trail.

**One correction to the item's citations.** The item named two
hardcoded-`'system'` sites — `apps/server/src/modules/resources/service.ts:170`
and `:260`. **There is a third at `:397`** (the same `task` kind in a list
projection). The published count does not change — still two of six _kinds_ —
but the set of sites was incomplete, and the criterion that cites it is
corrected rather than left wrong in a file that quotes it.

**The reading, published plainly.** "Authored by an agent" means: on the
resource side, `createdBy` carries the `agt_` id form; on the audit side,
`actorType` is `agent`. Neither `'owner'` nor `'system'` is an agent. The owner
may correct the reading later; the item does not wait on it.
