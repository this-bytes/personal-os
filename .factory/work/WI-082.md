# WI-082 — The autonomy policy that is actually enforced

**Owner:** Cog
**Verifier:** Probe (WI-085, independently)
**State:** implement — dispatched 2026-10-21 as part of the 48-hour build.
**Numbered:** 2026-10-21 by the session that owns the number space for this push.

## The problem

`autonomyPolicySchema` exists in `packages/contracts/src/common.ts:46` with
`mode`, `allowedActions`, `approvalRequired`, `maxTurns`, `maxDurationSeconds`,
`maxSpendUnits` and `dailySpendUnits`. It is stored on workspaces
(`apps/server/src/db/schema.ts:53`) and on goals (`:300`).

**Nothing reads it.** The only mentions outside the storage layer are the
workspace and goal write paths and a badge in the panel's settings. The limits
that are enforced today are the _conversation's own_ (`conversations.max_turns`,
`max_duration_seconds`, `max_spend_units`, `paused`) — a different object, on a
different scope, attached to a different thing.

`PRODUCT.md` §5 states the gap in the product's own words: **"The policy is
stored; it is not yet enforced."**

An "agent-native" OS whose autonomy policy is decorative is not the product
`PRODUCT.md` describes. This item makes the policy mean something.

## The decisions that are settled (do not re-open them)

1. **One resolution point.** A pure function in `packages/contracts/src/` that
   resolves the _effective_ policy for an execution from the workspace policy,
   the goal policy and the conversation's own safeguards. Every enforcement
   point consumes that one result. No enforcement point reads a policy row
   itself.
2. **Strictest wins.** For each numeric limit the effective value is the
   minimum across workspace, goal and conversation. `allowedActions` is the
   intersection. `approvalRequired` is the union. The effective `mode` is the
   _least_ autonomous of the three (`manual` < `assisted` < `autonomous`).
   A goal may tighten its workspace; it may never loosen it.
3. **Deny by default.** An action name that is not in the known vocabulary is
   **denied**. The vocabulary is a small, published set. An unknown action is a
   refusal, not a pass.
4. **Manual must not regress today.** Every workspace defaults to `manual`.
   An owner-directed turn, an owner-approved automation run and a triggered
   automation must keep working exactly as they do now. `manual` means "the
   agent may not _begin_ work on its own initiative" — not "the agent is
   switched off".
5. **Observable.** The effective policy is readable, so the panel and the MCP
   surface can show what an agent is actually permitted to do. A policy that
   cannot be read is a policy nobody can trust.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pure resolver in `packages/contracts/src/` — no database, no I/O —
      returns the effective policy from workspace + goal + conversation, and
      the strictest-wins rules above are unit-tested.
- [ ] The worker enforces the effective limits on a turn: turn count, duration
      and spend. A turn that exceeds a limit stops, and the outcome is recorded
      rather than silently truncated.
- [ ] An action outside the effective `allowedActions` is refused, with a
      structured error naming the action and the policy that refused it.
- [ ] An action inside `approvalRequired` cannot proceed without an owner
      decision, and the refusal-to-approval path is the same shape as the
      existing approval flow.
- [ ] The effective policy is readable over the API and in MCP, and a client
      can tell what an agent is permitted to do.
- [ ] Existing behaviour does not regress: the full suite is green, and the
      owner-directed chat, factory task and automation paths still work.

## Landed — 2026-10-21, Cog

**The policy is enforced now.** The strictest of the workspace's policy, the
goal's policy and the conversation's own safeguards is resolved in one pure
function — `resolveAutonomyPolicy` in `packages/contracts/src/autonomy.ts` —
and every enforcement point consumes that one result.

- **The resolver** (`packages/contracts/src/autonomy.ts`) is pure: no
  database, no I/O. The strictest limit wins, `allowedActions` is the
  intersection of the policies that name any, `approvalRequired` is their
  union, and the mode is the least autonomous of them. The action vocabulary
  is closed — `message.send`, `tool.call`, `artifact.create` — and an action
  the vocabulary does not know is denied.
- **The loader** (`apps/server/src/modules/autonomy/service.ts`) is the one
  place that knows which rows hold a policy. A goal or a conversation that
  belongs to another workspace has no say in this one.
- **The worker** (`apps/worker/src/executor.ts`) holds a turn to the effective
  policy: the chain's turn count and its spend before the turn starts, the
  duration as the deadline while it runs, and every action the agent
  announces. A turn that exceeds a limit stops, and the outcome is recorded —
  on the execution, on the reply, and in the audit trail.
- **The MCP surface** (`apps/server/src/modules/mcp/server.ts`) refuses an
  action the policy does not allow, and an action the policy wants the owner
  to approve is refused until the owner has decided. The request is raised in
  the same shape as the one an agent raises for itself.
- **It is readable**: `GET /api/autonomy-policy` and the `autonomy.read` MCP
  tool both return the effective policy, and where each value came from.

**Two additive contract additions, for Wren to publish**: the route `GET
/api/autonomy-policy` and the MCP tool `autonomy.read`. The published shapes
that already exist are unchanged.

**The mode is resolved and published, and nothing is gated by it yet.** The
only work in the system that begins on an agent's own initiative is an
agent's delegation, and
`apps/server/src/__tests__/coordinator-routing.test.ts` pins a delegation
dispatching a turn under the default (manual) policy — so gating it would
break the suite, and decision 4 pins the owner-directed turn, the
owner-approved automation run and the triggered automation. The mode is
carried through the resolver and is readable wherever the policy is; on its
own it is not an enforcement point.
