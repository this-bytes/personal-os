## The owner's answers, and the gap they name

The owner asked four questions on 2026-10-21, and answered all of them:

1. **Does the desktop experience become a first-class surface?** No. Web and
   PWA; MiniMax Code's desktop experience contributes interaction primitives.
2. **How does the domain structure get made real?** Declaratively — five
   domains, published as a map, no file moves yet.
3. **Does the instance's own substrate sit outside the agent-writable scope?**
   Yes. The owner accepts migrations; release is out of band.
4. **Does MIT open the door to depending on MiniMax Code?** No. Read and
   reimplement against our own contracts, attribute what is carried over, zero
   runtime dependencies.

And three more, in the second round: the whole MiniMax Code interaction model
is adopted at once; the factory roles join the control plane but keep their own
host; the harness patterns split by boundary. And three in the third: the
instance reaches the roles over MCP; two ledgers with one link each way; the
contract is specified in full. And one in the fourth: the reuse boundary is
read and reimplemented, and the licence is recorded.

**The gap.** `FACTORY.md` says a settled decision has a work item behind it, or
it is not a decision. Thirteen decisions had been settled in a conversation and
recorded nowhere. The stated scope also still said "interaction" alone, which no
longer describes the work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **The owner's decisions are written in their own words**, in
      `.factory/INTAKE.md`, with the reason each one was taken and what it costs.
- [ ] **The stated scope is amended** to name the three areas the factory now
      works in, and the reason — capacity — is recorded where FACTORY.md asks
      for it.
- [ ] **Every decision names the work item that carries it**, and every work
      item names the decision it carries. A decision with no work item behind it
      is not a decision, and the record has to show the pairing.
- [ ] **The wants are in the queue**, in the owner's words, with their verdicts.
- [ ] **Nothing here is a summary of the conversation.** The owner's own
      sentences are quoted; the factory's reading of them is marked as the
      factory's.
