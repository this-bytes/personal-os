# WI-033 — The audit trail records what happened, not that something happened

Opened by Chief, from the direction the owner chose
Numbered — WI-033, taken by the session that owns the number
space. WI-030, WI-031 and WI-032 are this session's; WI-024 through WI-029
are the other Chief session's. The numbers this session took are named in
`.factory/INTAKE.md`, so no session takes them twice.

## The problem

The product publishes a report on itself, and the numbers behind that report
are wrong in three ways at once.

**The audit entries record that something happened, not what happened.** Every
mutating route writes an entry, but the entry carries an assumed success
rather than the outcome. And **twelve of the thirty-four mutating routes write
no entry at all** — the ones that mutate through the service layer rather
than through a route handler of their own.

**No kind reports a real author.** `resources.createdBy` separates an agent's
authorship from the owner's for four of the six resource kinds, but
automations and tasks hardcode `createdBy: 'system'`
(`apps/server/src/modules/resources/service.ts:170` and `:260`), and their
tables have no `createdBy` column at all. For those two kinds the question
"was this authored by an agent?" is answered wrongly, not merely
unanswered.

**And the report itself is live.** WI-020 publishes the route that counts
what the product did. The first report the product gives the owner would be a
lie by omission: it would count agent authorship for four kinds out of six,
and count an audit trail that says nothing about outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Each of the thirty-four mutating routes writes an audit entry.** The
      twelve that wrote none write one, at the layer that knows the outcome.
- [ ] **An entry records whether the action succeeded.** An entry that says
      "done" when the action failed is worse than no entry, because it is
      evidence that is wrong.
- [ ] `POST /api/resources/:id/execute` records `queued`, not `done` — the
      action was accepted, not completed.
- [ ] **Automations and tasks carry the author who created them.** Their
      tables gain the column, or the author is read from where it already
      lives; the shape of that choice is the contract's business (WI-034).
- [ ] A test proves each of those three things.
