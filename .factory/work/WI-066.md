# WI-066 — The message shape publishes the fourth field the code carries

**Owner:** Wren
**Verifier:** Probe
**State:** implement — the code has the field, and the contract does not
publish it.
**Opened:** 2026-10-21 by Chief, from Cog's own report on WI-031, which named
the gap rather than inventing around it
**Numbered:** 2026-10-21 — **WI-066**, taken by the session that owns the
number space. WI-030 through WI-038, WI-044, WI-045, WI-062, WI-065 and WI-066
are this session's; WI-024 through WI-029 and WI-039 through WI-043 are the
other Chief session's, and WI-046 through WI-064 are the strategy session's.

## Problem

**`CONTRACTS.md`'s `### Additive extensions` section publishes three optional
fields on the `Message` shape. The code carries four.**

The contract says:

> The implementation adds three fields, all optional and all required by
> production behaviour:

and then lists `status`, `correlationId` and `origin`. **The code's `Message`
schema also has `detail`** — `detail: z.string().optional()`
(`packages/contracts/src/conversation.ts:90`) — and the contract does not
publish it.

**Cog found this and named it rather than working around it.** Its report on
WI-031 says: _"The implementation adds a fourth additive extension to the
published `Message` shape — `detail?: string`, the server's sentence, shown as
given — and the published 'Additive extensions' section says the implementation
adds three. **That gap is the contract's, not mine; I am not inventing a second
shape and moving on, I am naming it.**"_

**It is WI-031's own acceptance criterion that is unmet.** WI-031 asks that
**"the published shape matches the code exactly, member for member — checked
against the implementation, not against the proposal above."** Four fields
against three is not member for member, and **until this is closed WI-031
cannot be accepted**, however good the code is.

**The field is wanted.** WI-031's criterion _"the reason reaches the panel"_ is
served by it: the server's sentence about a closed turn travels on the reply,
and the client shows what it is given. **The fix is to publish the field, not
to remove it** — the sentence is the point.

- [ ] **`### Additive extensions` publishes four fields, not three**, and the

      actually carries; do not take the field list from this file or from
      Cog's report.**
- [ ] **Each field says what it is for**, in the way the other three do, and
      the new one says it is the server's own sentence about the turn, not
      something the client rewrites.
- [ ] **The route's entry says what a client written before it does** with a
      reply that carries `detail`, and what a reply recorded before the change
      means — it carries no `detail`, and that is not an error.
- [ ] **Nothing is reclassified.** A reply written before the change is still
      the reply it was, and no existing record is rewritten.
- [ ] **The shape is member for member with the code**, checked against
      `packages/contracts/src/conversation.ts` and not against a proposal.
- [ ] **The neighbouring entries are not contradicted** — the message entry, the
      route table's row, the waiting list's entries, and the `unknown` outcome
      entry.
- [ ] **WI-031's route entry and this section agree.** WI-031's entry publishes
      the route; this section publishes the shape it returns.

## Notes

**Why the contract is fixed rather than the code.** The contract leads the code,
and the published shape is what a client is written against. **The field
carries the server's sentence, and WI-031's criterion requires the reason to
reach the panel.** Removing the field to make the contract true would remove
the reason from the panel — fixing the symptom and losing the feature.

**Why this item exists at all, and why it is not a defect report.** The
implementer found it and named it in its report, which is the behaviour the
factory wants. **The gap it found is the contract's, and the contract is
Wren's.** An implementer that widened the published contract itself would have
made the promise and kept it in the same session, which is the one thing the
separation of the contract half from the code half exists to prevent.
