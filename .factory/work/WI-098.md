## The problem

ADR 0007 quotes the Forge boundary rule from `prototypes/DIRECTION.md` at
`:45-48`, with **one** declared exception, the Needs-you rail.

The direction record was amended on 2026-10-22 at `d3ef0d2` and now publishes
the rule with **two** declared exceptions, the Needs-you rail and the
constellation, at `:51-55`.

The ADR is where a reader looks for decisions, and it was quoting a rule the
direction record no longer holds. The quote is the load-bearing part: a rule
quoted without its load-bearing exception is what put the landing page in the
wrong room in WI-024 and WI-040.

## What this item does

Re-sync ADR 0007 to the direction record. **The rule is unchanged in
substance; only its declared exceptions grew.**

**Four places, not three.**

1. `:157-161`, the quote block — replaced with the rule verbatim from
   `prototypes/DIRECTION.md:51-55`. The verb becomes plural ("carry"),
   because there are two.
2. `:163-173`, the provenance note — the rule now stands at `:51-55`, not
   `:45-48`, and there are two repairs behind it (`e560165` and `d3ef0d2`).
   The note's account is kept: a rule must never be quoted without its
   load-bearing exception.
3. `:175-184`, the paragraph under the quote — it said "There is one
   deliberate crossing". There are two, and the second is the constellation,
   which carries the connect decision. The reason is given once: **a decision
   is taken where the list is.**
4. `:231-240`, **found by the sweep and not named in the brief.** The
   paragraph headed "The one consequence the direction leaves open" said the
   connect decision had to live somewhere in place — "either the constellation
   carries it, or the agent list keeps a decision surface in the execution
   room". That was true before `d3ef0d2`: the pre-repair direction record
   does not contain the word "connect" at all. It is false now, and it is the
   same defect, in the same file, caused by the same commit — so it is
   corrected here rather than reported. Leaving it would have left the ADR
   contradicting the direction record on exactly the point this item re-syncs.
