# WI-035 — Resources keep a history the owner can take back

Opened by Chief, from the direction the owner chose
Numbered — WI-035, taken by the session that owns the number
space. WI-030, WI-031 and WI-032 are this session's; WI-024 through WI-029
are the other Chief session's.

## The problem

A resource is overwritten when it is changed. The owner cannot take a
resource back to the version they had before an agent rewrote it, and the
product does not keep the earlier version, so "take it back" is not a feature
that could be added later — the history is gone.

This is **C-21, scoped to resources**: the owner taking something back. It is
the largest of the seven wants and it is deliberately narrow, because the
wider question — the owner taking back _anything_ the agents did — is a
different and larger question, and narrowing it is what makes it buildable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **A resource's revisions are retrievable.** Each revision of a resource
      is retained rather than overwritten, and the retained revisions can be
      read.
- [ ] **The restore route is published**, in the route table, with the
      revision it restored.
- [ ] **The restore is audited like any other change.** The audit trail says
      the restore happened, and says which revision it restored.
- [ ] A test proves a restore returns the resource to the earlier revision's
      spec, and that the audit trail says the restore happened.

## Notes

**Why it is deferred rather than refused.** The triage's words: _the furthest
from the owner's stated priority and the largest of the seven_. The owner said
what the panel must carry — interaction, and "what needs me" on the landing
page. A restore route for a resource is neither. The item is written and
numbered so the deferral is a queue decision with an owner, not a hole.

**One file, one live item.** The route table, the resource model and the audit
trail are shared with WI-033. **This item does not start until WI-033 has
landed**, and the two are not in the same worktree at the same time.
