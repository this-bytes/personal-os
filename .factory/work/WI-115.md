## The finding

`docs/resource-templates.md:66-70` says, of the agent path:

> Separately, automations and tasks report `createdBy: 'system'` regardless of who
> created them (`apps/server/src/modules/resources/service.ts:203,293,448`), so for those
> two kinds the product's own measure of agent authorship is **wrong, not merely
> uncounted** (PRODUCT.md §7's own words).

**Both halves are wrong on today's tree.**

**The three cited lines carry nothing of the claim:**

| cited line | reads |
| ---------- | ----- |
| `service.ts:203` | `},` — the close of the table provider's `get` |
| `service.ts:293` | `action: AutomationAction;` |
| `service.ts:448` | `* The registry of templates.` — a comment |

**And the defect the sentence describes has been fixed.** The author is now read from
the record, with `system` only as the fallback for a resource nobody authored:

- the automation's projection reads the row's own author —
  `createdBy: row.createdBy,` at `service.ts:234`;
- the task's author is resolved by
  `factory.taskAuthor(id) ?? RESOURCE_AUTHOR_SYSTEM` at `service.ts:345`, and the
  projection writes `createdBy: authors.get(task.id) ?? RESOURCE_AUTHOR_SYSTEM,` at
  `service.ts:513`;
- `RESOURCE_AUTHOR_SYSTEM` is `'system'` and lives in
  `packages/contracts/src/resource.ts`.

**WI-020 — "the author's own name, on the row" — is what fixed it, and it landed.**
So the guide describes a defect that is closed, tells the reader the product's measure
of agent authorship is "wrong, not merely uncounted", and cites three lines that never
carried the claim.

**This is the vehicle's defect, not WI-108's.** The guide is the publication's
(`WI-111`), and it landed with the vehicle. It is numbered here because the lead found
it while measuring WI-114's pointers, and a finding that is not numbered is a finding
that is lost.
