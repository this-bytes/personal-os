## The problem

**Three places in the product record still say the owner has not decided, and
the owner has.** `PRODUCT.md` is the document a new reader opens first, and a
record that asks for a word the owner has already given is the one thing it
cannot carry.

**§7 says the measures are "waiting on the owner"** (`PRODUCT.md:201-203`). The
owner approved the measures route at 06:01 on 2026-10-21. It is WI-020, and it
is in flight.

**§7 says the panel "shows both" measures** (`PRODUCT.md:176`). WI-020's ruling
of 2026-10-21 is that the band shows the first measure's counts and **names the
second as not yet countable** — the audit trail records that something happened,
not what happened. The record has to say the same thing, or it states the
opposite of the decision.

**§10 says "The owner is being asked to settle it"** (`PRODUCT.md:266`). The
owner settled the protocol boundary on 2026-10-20: the panel's agent-facing
surface conforms to MCP and AG-UI, ACP and A2A are the agent-connection side,
and no protocol is forked. That is in `FACTORY.md` as settled doctrine.

**§9 says "The owner has not chosen it yet"** (`PRODUCT.md:226-228`). The same
2026-10-20 answer chose the panel's agent-facing surfaces. What the owner has
**not** ratified is MCP Apps for agent-created UI, and that half of the section
stays a recommendation.

      and **keeps MCP Apps as a proposal and a recommendation the owner has not

      doctrine that holds it.
- [ ] **Every claim is one the owner actually made**, and nothing in these
      sections is decided in the owner's name. A record that fixes a false claim
      and quietly makes a new one is worse than the false claim.
- [ ] **Nothing else in the product record is changed in the same item.**

## Notes

**This is the same class of fix as WI-037.** WI-037 corrects the record's
statement of the direction; this one corrects the record's statement of what is
still open. They are two items because one file carries one live item, and they
are one class because a record is only as good as the truth of what it says is
undecided.

**Why §9 is in it even though half of it is still true.** The sentence "the
owner has not chosen it yet" is false where the boundary is concerned and true
where MCP Apps is concerned. A section cannot be half-denying a decision, so
the fix is to separate the two halves and say which is which.
