## The problem

`docs/decisions/0007-the-environment-agents-operate-and-extend.md` has a
section called **"The visual direction"**, and it says:

> The panel's visual direction is **Desk (B)**, recommended by Vision and **not
> yet decided**. The owner has one word to say: confirm B, or name A, C or D.

**The owner said their word.** On 2026-10-21 at 06:01 they chose **D — Forge**,
against the factory's recommendation of B — Desk.

So the product's decision record currently states the opposite of the decision,
and it states it in the present tense, as a question still waiting for an
answer. A decision record that contradicts the decision is worse than no
section at all: it is a record that will be read, and quoted, and believed.

## Why now

The owner answered, and the answer was recorded immediately in
`.factory/INTAKE.md` and in [WI-010](WI-010.md). The ADR is the product's own
decision record — the place where a reader finds out what was decided and
what it cost — and it is the last place that still tells the old story.

The direction record itself is at
[`prototypes/DIRECTION.md`](../../prototypes/DIRECTION.md). The ADR is a
section of it, not a second document.

## What the owner chose, and what it cost

**D — Forge.** The recommendation was **B — Desk**, and the factory concurred
with it: the owner's own Q2 answer is that the human decides, and Desk is the
only direction whose home screen is that decision list. The owner chose the
direction the recommendation rejected, and that is the owner's call to make.

**The boundary Forge leaves open was settled**, and it is the rule: _if it is
a picture of the system, it is in the orientation room; if it is a place where
work is inspected or decided, it is in the execution room._ The constellation
and the agent list are in the orientation room — the constellation **is** the
agent list, spatialised, and building it twice would build the same information
twice. The factory board and the automation runs are in the execution room.
The dashboards are in the orientation room. The Map's **Needs you** rail is the
one deliberate crossing, and it is load-bearing: it carries the decision
controls, so a decision is taken where the list is.

**The cost is structural, not cosmetic**: a new component (the constellation),
a third surface (the dashboards), a room switch, and a material that re-hues
rather than replaces. The numbers are in `prototypes/DIRECTION.md`, and the
build item is [WI-024](WI-024.md).
