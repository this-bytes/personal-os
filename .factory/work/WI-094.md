## The lead's second addition — 2026-10-22 01:58: merging the briefs found a third red, and it is the pin's defect

**Merging the briefs took the pin from two reds to three.** The third is
_"puts every surface in the room the direction document puts it in"_, and the

```
AssertionError: brief 01 puts "The factory board" in the wrong room:

```

**The brief is right and the matcher is brittle.** The case at
`apps/web/src/__tests__/forge-briefs.test.ts:312-314` finds the row and then
asserts the **rendered line** contains the **rendered cell** `| Execution |` — and
Cog's corrected brief 01 is a **column-padded** table, so the cell is

assumes a formatting instead of reading the structure.** A markdown table's
alignment is not a claim about the product, and a gate that goes red when
somebody aligns a column is a gate that trains people to ignore it.

**So the pin must parse the row into cells** — split the line on `|`, trim each
cell, and compare the room cell — **not match rendered strings.** And the same
goes for the document's own table at `prototypes/DIRECTION.md:57-63`, which the
same case reads the same brittle way.

**Nothing else changed.** The briefs are correct: the factory board is in the
execution room, and the document agrees. **The red is the pin's.**
