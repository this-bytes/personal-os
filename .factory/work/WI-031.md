## The problem

The contract promises one thing about an unknown outcome that the code cannot
keep for a turn. `CONTRACTS.md:125-128`:

> **An `unknown` item is never retried automatically.** There is no reaper, no
> redispatch and no reconciliation pass. An unknown outcome is a question for the
> owner, and only the owner can close it. Every transition into and out of
> `unknown` is written to the audit trail.

For a **task** that promise is kept: the owner resolves one with
`PATCH /api/tasks/:id`, the task leaves `unknown`, and the transition is
written to the audit trail as `task.outcome.resolved` — which is what
`apps/server/src/__tests__/unknown-outcome.test.ts` ("leaves the audit trail
when the owner resolves it") pins.

For a **turn** it is not kept, and it cannot be:

- `closeReply` (`apps/server/src/modules/approvals/resume.ts:186-188`) sets the
  messages of a stopped execution to `status: 'failed'` and nothing else. The
  reason lives only in the audit trail, and the panel surfaces no audit trail,
  so the owner is left with a red "failed" and no sentence.
  **This is the same gap Cog named on WI-007's report** (2026-10-21, commit
  `6878e7c`): the reply in the conversation carries a bare `failed` badge with
  no sentence, and it is not the client's to close — the reason has to reach
  the message first. **Chief's ruling: it belongs here**, and Probe was asked
  to check that ruling in WI-007's verification (`task_2e431d7647e5`).
- `resolveExecution`
  (`apps/server/src/modules/approvals/service.ts:160-165`) is a **service**
  method, called from exactly one place — the approval decision path
  (`resume.ts:151`). **There is no owner-facing route that resolves an
  execution**, so the owner has no way to close a turn whose outcome could not
  be confirmed, and no published route to close it with.

**This is a drift that exists today, and WI-027 makes it visible.** The
contract says an unknown outcome reaches the owner as _"check this"_ and that
only the owner can close it. WI-027 puts unknown turns on the waiting list, so
after it lands the owner sees a turn to check and cannot close it. A visible
dead end is worse than an invisible one, which is why this item exists rather
than being folded into WI-027.

**Cog named this, rather than fixing it.** Its WI-007 report (`6878e7c`) says
the second symptom "cannot be fixed in the client", and that the reason "has
to reach the message before the client has anything to show, so this is a
server-side gap needing its own item — named rather than folded in here". This
is that item.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner can close a turn whose outcome could not be confirmed, through a
      published route, and the turn leaves the waiting list when they do.
- [ ] The route carries the owner's conclusion — _it did run_ or _it did not
      run_ — rather than assuming one. The product does not know what happened;
      the owner does, and the route records what they said.
- [ ] The transition into and out of `unknown` is written to the audit trail,
      the way the task path's `task.outcome.resolved` is, and the audit entry
      names the owner.
- [ ] **The reason reaches the panel.** A turn that was closed rather than
      continued says so in the panel, with the sentence the server produced —
      the same rule WI-007 established for a decision, applied to the reply
      that the decision closed. The client shows what it is given and does not
      reword it.
- [ ] The published contract carries the route, what it does, and a **migration
      note**: what a client written before it does, and what it means for a
      turn recorded before the change. Nothing is reclassified, and no
      execution is closed by this change.
- [ ] The route is **additive**: no existing route's shape or semantics change,
      it takes the same auth and workspace rules as every other owner-facing
      route, and it is published in the route table with the rest.
- [ ] The published shape matches the code exactly, member for member — checked
      against the implementation, not against the proposal above.
- [ ] A test pins it, and the test is Probe's to write
      (`**/*.test.ts` is Probe's file, by the ownership table). The test says:
      an unknown turn reaches the owner, the owner closes it, and the audit
      trail says so. It fails before the change and passes after.
- [ ] `npm run typecheck`, `npm run test`, `npm run format:check`,
