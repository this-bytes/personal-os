## WI-071 — The panel's accent means one thing, and the caution colour is freed

The code is Cog’s; the documentation half is Wren’s.
Numbered — WI-071, taken by this session as the next free number above all allocated ones. **WI-069 was taken by the number space owner in `8f87d87` while this item was being written, so this item was renumbered from WI-070 and the collision is recorded here.** WI-030–WI-038, WI-044, WI-045, WI-062 and WI-065–WI-069 are the number space owner's; WI-024–WI-029 and WI-039–WI-043 belong to the other Chief session; WI-046–WI-064 are the strategy session's. If another session has taken WI-071 in the meantime, this item is renumbered again and the table is corrected.

### Problem

PRODUCT.md:270-276 states the rule the panel has not kept: one accent colour, and it always means the same thing, which is live or waiting on you. Structure and data stay in their own colours. PRODUCT.md:278-284 then says so plainly — the panel does not do this yet. Its accent is indigo, and indigo marks structure rather than liveness, and the event stream's state is a four-colour traffic light: emerald for live, amber for reconnecting, red for offline, slate for idle.

Amber is the collision that breaks the rule, because in Forge amber always means live or waiting on you and never caution. Today amber means caution, interruption and liveness at the same time, in ten lines across six files, and it reaches a further eight places through a badge variant that never names a colour at all.

The token system cannot express the rule. `apps/web/src/index.css:12-38` holds one `@theme` block with a slate scale (`apps/web/src/index.css:13-23`) and an indigo scale (`apps/web/src/index.css:25-35`) and nothing else: there is no token for live, none for waiting on you, none for caution, and none for error. The rule cannot be kept because the vocabulary to keep it in does not exist.

### Why now

This is the most visible change in the window, and it is the one the owner already decided: the direction is **D — Forge**, chosen on 2026-10-21 (PRODUCT.md:282-284). The briefs and the direction document are written and authoritative; what is missing is the application to the shipped panel.

It is also the cheapest of the four to get wrong in a way nobody notices. A number that reads as decoration still reads as decoration, and the panel is the product.

### Notes

**What "the accent" means here.** PRODUCT.md:270-276 says one accent, always live or waiting on you, with structure and data in their own colours. That is the rule the change has to satisfy, and the token the rule names is the one that has to be unambiguous. The current indigo (apps/web/src/components/AppShell.tsx:92, `:120`, and twenty-odd other lines) is structure, and it stops being the accent.

**Where the caution colour goes.** `prototypes/briefs/02-material.md:58-67` gives the per-site treatment: the two conflict and blocked notices are failures and take the error colour, the paused notice is a state and takes the neutral scale, the stream dot and the interrupted badge take the neutral scale because a feed in flux is not the owner being needed, the degraded connection takes the neutral scale for the same reason, and the `warning` badge variant takes the error colour for a warning that is a warning.

**The badge variant keeps its name.** `prototypes/briefs/02-material.md:98-100`: the `warning` variant in `packages/ui/src/card.tsx` is what it is called, not what it is coloured, and only its colour changes. A variant named `warning` rendered in the error colour is not a contradiction.

**The `warn` token is not a second amber.** `prototypes/briefs/02-material.md:69-72`: it is declared with the same value as `live` and it must not be reached for out of habit. If a future surface needs a caution colour, it uses the error colour or the neutral scale.

**An honest limit.** The shipped panel's palette is not the prototype's. The prototype's variables live in `prototypes/d-forge/theme.css`, and the panel's live in `apps/web/src/index.css`, and the two are reconciled by hand rather than by a build step. The report says what was carried over and what was left, because the next person will otherwise assume the two files agree.

**Who writes the tests.** Every test file belongs to Probe (FACTORY.md:52). The e2e specs in `apps/web/e2e` assert no colours today, so the palette change should not disturb them, and they still have to pass.
