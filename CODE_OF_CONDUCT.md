# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can disagree about the
architecture, argue about a contract, and say "I think this is wrong" without
being made to feel unwelcome for it.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that make this project open to
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- **Being precise.** Disagreeing with a decision is welcome; the decision is
  argued from the code, the contract and the requirement, not from rank.
- **Assuming good faith.** Someone asking a basic question is not someone
  wasting your time.
- **Giving credit.** Say whose work you are building on.
- **Being patient with people who are learning.** This project has a lot of
  moving parts and a steep first hour.
- **Accepting responsibility.** If you break something, say so plainly and
  fix it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without their explicit
  permission;
- deliberately misleading others about a defect, a capability or a benchmark;
- sustained disruption of discussions;
- dismissing or belittling a report of a security problem;
- encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in
public.

It also applies to the autonomous agents that work on this repository. An
agent's output is the project's output while it acts on the project, and the
same standard of precision and honesty applies to it: an agent that reports
work as passing when it did not pass is making a false statement, and that is
a conduct problem as much as a technical one.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately. Reports are
handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and
security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the other maintainers.

Maintainers are expected to resolve reports in a way that is fair and
proportionate. Possible responses include:

- a private correction;
- a public warning;
- a temporary ban from any sort of interaction;
- a permanent ban.
