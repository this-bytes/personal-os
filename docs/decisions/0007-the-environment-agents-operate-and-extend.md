# 7. An agent-operated environment with chat as the work surface

**Status:** accepted; updated by the owner's 2026-10-22 recovery decision.

Personal OS owns interaction, coordination and creation for one human owner.
External agents own their reasoning and execution. The owner directs, approves
and accepts; an agent's completion report does not establish acceptance.

Rich conversations are the primary place to direct work, inspect activity and
outputs, answer questions and make decisions. Keep Forge styling; the map, board
and dashboards support the conversation. Deliver rich chat across harnesses before
building autonomous teams and the product factory.

The earlier map-first layout and fixed-role development process created competing
priorities and handoffs without demonstrating useful work. They are retired. The
full prior record remains in Git at 507708b. Current requirements are in PRODUCT.md
and [the recovery decisions](0018-the-chat-first-recovery-decisions.md);
implementation follows [the roadmap](../roadmap.md).
